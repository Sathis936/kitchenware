Metallurgist's Quick Kitchenware Tips

Essential do's and don'ts to keep your stainless steel shining like new for 30+ years without toxic chemicals.

💡

Removing Rainbow Tint

Rainbow or heat tint is caused by natural chromium oxide thickening under high stovetop temperatures. It is completely food-safe. A splash of white vinegar or lemon juice wiped with a soft cloth restores original silver luster instantly.

⚠️

Preventing Thermal Shock

Never submerge a scalding-hot stainless steel pan directly into cold sink water. Sudden thermal contraction can loosen the encapsulated tri-ply base or warp the cooking surface. Always allow the cookware to cool naturally first.

🧂

Salt Pitting Prevention

Always add cooking salt to water after it has reached a rolling boil, or stir immediately to dissolve. Undissolved coarse salt resting on cold steel can cause tiny permanent pitting marks on the interior surface.

✨

Baking Soda Base Cleanse

For stubborn burnt oil or curry drippings baked onto the exterior bottom, apply a paste of baking soda and warm water with a few drops of dish soap. Let sit for 15 minutes, then wipe with a non-scratch scrubber for factory brilliance.
