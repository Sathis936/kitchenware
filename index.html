Culinary Engineering

Tri-Ply Bonded Metallurgy & Thermal Science

Why professional chefs and home cooks choose tri-ply stainless steel over conventional aluminium or non-stick Teflon cookware.

Proprietary 3-Layer Bonding

Zero Hotspots. 100% Food Purity.

100% Induction Compatible Dishwasher & Oven Safe
1
SS-304 Surgical Interior (18/10)

Food contact surface crafted from certified AISI 304 food-grade stainless steel with 18% Chromium and 10% Nickel. Completely non-reactive to acidic gravies, tomato curries, and citrus marinades. Retains authentic flavors without metallic leaching.

2
Pure Aluminum Core (99.5%)

Sandwiched high-density aluminum thermal layer running from the base up through the rim sidewalls. Conducts heat 400% faster than standard steel, distributing uniform warmth and eliminating burned food centers and stubborn scorching.

3
SS-430 Magnetic Exterior Base

Heavy-gauge ferritic magnetic stainless steel exterior compatible with all modern cooking platforms: electromagnetic induction cooktops, gas ranges, radiant electric coils, and ceramic cookers. Resists base warping even under high-heat searing.
